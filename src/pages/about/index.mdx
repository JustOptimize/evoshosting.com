## Our Story

EvosHosting was founded in 2023 with the goal of offering quality hosting services at affordable prices. Our aim is to provide excellent hosting service to everyone, regardless of budget.

## Where Are We Located?

Our servers are located in the SkyLink DataCenter in the Netherlands. Particular attention has been paid to security and redundancy, with air-circulated cooling and airflow optimization. The DataCenter is strategically positioned, allowing high-speed connections to the main network nodes (AMS-IX and DE-CIX), ensuring low latency across Europe.

Our DataCenter is also equipped with the latest physical security technologies, with 24/7 surveillance, controlled access, and advanced detection systems to ensure maximum protection of the data hosted on our servers.

## Anti-DDoS Protection

The security of our customers is an absolute priority for us. For this reason, we offer anti-DDoS protection, partially developed by us, ensuring that websites hosted on our servers are protected from external attacks.

Our DDoS defense system is designed to detect and mitigate threats in real-time, keeping your site always online and operational.

With a capacity of up to 1.5Tbps, our Anti-DDoS system allows us to protect our customers from attacks of any size and complexity. This advanced system is able to analyze incoming traffic and filter any suspicious activity, ensuring continuous protection without compromising service performance.
